# Resources: NASA Space Apps 2026 - "Be An Earth System Trend Detective!"

## NASA Data & Resources
- [NASA Power](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/): Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources. Solar radiation and meteorological variables (temperature, precipitation, humidity, wind, etc.) at hourly, daily, monthly and climatology resolution, accessible for a point, region or global grid through a web interface and a REST API.
- [Giovanni](https://giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov/): Menus of numerous data variables; generates and downloads time series.
- [Hydrology Time Series](https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/information/tools?title=Hydrology%20Time%20Series): Time series generation with hydrological data.
- [AppEEARS](https://appeears.earthdatacloud.nasa.gov/): Access and transform geospatial data from federal archives; includes time-series generation.
- [Climate Data Online (CDO)](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/cdo-web/): NCDC archive of global historical weather and climate data, with a mapping tool.
- [Time-Series Analysis of Remotely-Sensed SeaWiFS Chlorophyll in River-Influenced Coastal Regions](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20090042747/downloads/20090042747.pdf): Research article demonstrating time-series creation and significance testing.

## Space Agency Partner Resources
- [KNMI Climate Explorer](https://climexp.knmi.nl/): Web-based tool to explore, analyze and visualize climate data.
- [Statistical test methods to detect trends in time series data](https://medium.com/@snp.kriss/statistical-test-methods-to-detect-trends-in-time-series-data-b361a0ec530b): Article about testing trends in data.
- [A Comprehensive Guide to Statistical Significance](https://www.statsig.com/perspectives/a-comprehensive-guide-to-statistical-significance): Guide to significance testing.

## CSA (Canadian Space Agency)
- [Radarsat Constellation Mission (RCM) - Data](https://donnees-data.asc-csa.gc.ca/en/dataset/rcm): Maritime surveillance, disaster management, ecosystem monitoring.
- [Geo.ca](http://geo.ca/): Canada's open geospatial information.
- [SWOT Mission - Information](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/satellites/swot/): Surface water elevation of lakes, rivers, reservoirs and oceans.
- [Monitoring and protecting our ecosystem from space](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/satellites/everyday-lives/monitoring-and-protecting-our-ecosystems-from-space.asp): Satellite data for ecosystem and resource monitoring.
- [Synthetic Aperture Radar Python Tutorial](https://github.com/asc-csa/Synthetic_Aperture_Radar_Tutorial): Working with SAR imagery using Python and QGIS.

# WOMM Earth: demo video storyboard (30 s, silent walkthrough)

**What it is:** a NASA data-detective on a 3D globe. Pick a variable, places and years, press Investigate, and get an honest trend verdict, then dig further.
**For:** students and curious people who want to know if a place is really changing, and how sure we can be.
**Sets it apart:** two trend tests and one honest verdict ("Not proven yet" is a real answer); correlation; a multivariable radar; Gaia, an AI helper that only quotes numbers the engine computed.
**Visual hook:** the silver-on-black globe with Belgium and the Netherlands lit up, plus the question.
**Tone:** polished and calm, a product walkthrough. No music or sound, so the captions carry the story.
**Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## The case (real data, NASA POWER / MERRA-2)
- **Trend:** Air temperature, Belgium + Netherlands, 2000–2020, Annual.
  - Belgium: +0.44 °C/decade, Not proven yet.
  - Netherlands: +0.35 °C/decade, Not proven yet.
- **Correlation:** temperature vs sunlight at ground, Belgium, monthly anomalies. r = 0.36 raw, 0.34 detrended, p < 0.001.
- **Multivariable comparison:** air temperature, rain and snow, sunlight at ground.
- **Gaia:** "What does 'Not proven yet' mean here?" Real answer captured from production.
